OurDailyWalk ("the App") is provided by U.U.N.Z. ("we", "us", or "our"). It is one of F.B. Meyer's most loved work, and we hope it will bless. comfort and encourage you in your life journey.

We respect your privacy. OurDailyWalk is designed so that we do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information or other user data.

1. Information We Collect

OurDailyWalk does not collect personal information or personally identifiable information from users.

The App does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, telephone number, physical address, or other personal information.

We do not collect information such as your location, contacts, photographs, audio recordings, browsing history, device identifiers, or other personal or sensitive information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

If OurDailyWalk stores information locally on your device for the App to function, that information remains on your device and is not transmitted to us.

We do not have access to information stored locally by the App on your device.

3. How We Use Information

Because OurDailyWalk does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not use personal information for advertising, marketing, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share personal information with third parties.

5. Advertising

OurDailyWalk does not contain advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs to collect information about users.

6. Analytics and Tracking

OurDailyWalk does not use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or tracking technologies to collect information about users.

7. Third-Party Services

OurDailyWalk does not use third-party services to collect or process personal information on our behalf.

8. Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information held by us that requires storage or transmission security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not collect or retain personal information about users.

Any information that may be stored locally by the App on your device is under your control and is not accessible to us. Removing the App or clearing its local application data will remove locally stored App data, subject to the behavior of your Android device and any Android backup functionality you may have enabled.

10. Children's Privacy

OurDailyWalk does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality or data practices of OurDailyWalk change.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
